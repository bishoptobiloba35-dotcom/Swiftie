# SwiftDrop Privacy Notice (Draft)

**Last updated:** 28 September 2026

This draft is intended for product implementation and must be reviewed and completed by the operator's Nigerian privacy/legal adviser before public launch.

## Information we process

SwiftDrop may process:
- account details such as name, phone number and email;
- sender and receiver details needed to complete a delivery;
- pickup and drop-off addresses and coordinates;
- driver GPS location while an active delivery is being performed;
- parcel and delivery information;
- pickup-condition photographs;
- payment, escrow, refund and payout records;
- support requests, disputes, reviews and operational events;
- device push-notification tokens.

## Why we process it

We use information to create and manage deliveries, calculate quotations, process payments, hold and release escrow, match and monitor couriers, provide live tracking, verify delivery with a receiver PIN, prevent fraud and abuse, provide support, resolve disputes, maintain security, and meet legal or regulatory obligations.

## Location

Driver location is used for active delivery tracking. Background location is enabled for the driver application only while required for an active delivery workflow. Customer location is requested only when the customer chooses to use their current location for pickup.

## Photographs

A driver may capture a pickup photograph as delivery evidence. Pickup photographs and driver KYC documents are stored privately and are exposed only through authorization-controlled application endpoints.

## Payments

Payment processing is performed through the configured payment provider. SwiftDrop should not store raw card credentials. Payment, escrow, refund and payout records are retained for operational, accounting, dispute and compliance purposes.

## Sharing

Information may be shared with the parties necessary to perform a delivery, payment and payout providers, infrastructure/service providers acting on SwiftDrop's instructions, support personnel, regulators or law-enforcement authorities where legally required, and other recipients expressly disclosed to the data subject.

## Security

SwiftDrop uses authentication controls, access restrictions, private object storage, rate limiting, delivery-scoped authorization, encrypted transport requirements for production origins, and operational audit records. No security measure can guarantee absolute security.

## Retention

Information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the stated purposes, contractual and accounting requirements, dispute handling, fraud prevention, and applicable Nigerian legal obligations. Final retention schedules should be approved by the operator's privacy/legal adviser.

## Data-subject rights

Subject to applicable law and lawful processing grounds, users may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and other data-protection rights. Requests should be directed to the operator's published privacy contact.
