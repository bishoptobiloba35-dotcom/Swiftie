**Last updated:** 28 September 2026

## 1. Service

SwiftDrop is a technology platform for arranging delivery and courier services between customers, recipients and approved service providers. SwiftDrop may use third-party payment, mapping, communications, cloud and other infrastructure providers.

## 2. User responsibilities

Users must provide accurate account, recipient, address and parcel information, keep authentication credentials secure, and use the service lawfully. Users must not submit prohibited, dangerous, unlawful or inadequately packaged items.

## 3. Delivery information

Quotes are calculated from information supplied by the customer and the platform's pricing rules. A final payable amount is shown before payment. Users should verify addresses, parcel dimensions, weight and recipient details before confirming an order.

## 4. Payment and escrow

Where escrow is offered, the customer's payment may be held after successful payment authorization and remains subject to the delivery workflow. Release is triggered by the receiver's verified confirmation or an authorized dispute-resolution outcome. Refunds remain subject to payment-provider processing and applicable law.

## 5. Pickup evidence and tracking

The courier may photograph the parcel at pickup. During an active delivery, the driver application may transmit location information so authorized tracking participants can see delivery progress. Location reporting can be affected by device, network, GPS and environmental limitations.

## 6. Receiver confirmation

A delivery is designed to be completed when the receiver confirms receipt using the delivery PIN and verified receiver information. Users must not disclose the PIN except to the intended receiver.

## 7. Reviews

Users may submit delivery reviews. Reviews must be truthful, relevant and non-abusive. SwiftDrop may moderate or remove content that violates applicable rules.

## 8. Disputes and support

Customers, drivers and verified receivers may use the support and dispute workflows available in the application. SwiftDrop may review delivery events, tracking information, pickup evidence, payment records and other relevant information when resolving a dispute.

## 9. Prohibited conduct

Users must not use SwiftDrop for illegal goods, fraud, harassment, unauthorized surveillance, impersonation, payment abuse, account compromise, or other unlawful or harmful activity.

## 10. Service availability

The platform may experience outages, maintenance, GPS limitations, network failures, provider failures or other interruptions. SwiftDrop should not be represented as guaranteeing uninterrupted service or a particular delivery time unless expressly agreed.
